A first-person shooter in the style of modern military multiplayer games, built to run in a browser. It's one self-contained `index.html` using three.js, with no build step and no backend. Every texture, model and sound is generated in code.

## What you can do

- **Practice vs Bots:** 3v3 Team Deathmatch against bots, offline. Bots come in three skill levels (Recruit, Regular, Veteran) that change reaction time, aim, recoil control and how well they see and hear.
- **Play Online:** host or join a 3v3 room with other people, peer to peer, with a lobby and room browser.
- **Firing Range:** free practice with every weapon.
- **Create-a-Class:** pick a weapon and up to two attachments, plus grenades. XP, levels and unlocks track your progress.

## The arsenal

Six weapons, each tuned separately: two assault rifles, an SMG, a pump shotgun, a bolt-action sniper and a pistol. Attachments include a reflex sight, a 3x scope, a suppressor that keeps you off the enemy minimap, an extended magazine and a vertical grip. There are frag grenades you can cook and flashbangs, and killstreaks.

## How far along it is

I built it in phases and I'm through six of seven: the map and gun feel, all weapons, bots and the match rules, classes and unlocks, killstreaks, and multiplayer. The last phase is polish: performance, balance and menus.
