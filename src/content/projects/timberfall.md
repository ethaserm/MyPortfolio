*Walk the clearing. Fell the trees. Stop walking.*

Timberfall is an isometric game that runs in the browser. You play a woodcutter in a clearing: chop trees, carry what you gather (the pack holds 20), and bring it back to your camp. You move with WASD or by dragging, and walking into a glowing ring opens a building.

## The clearing

The map has an old oak, a crafting bench, a depot, a storehouse and a market on the road east, plus empty sites marked out for a sawmill and a forge, and a collapsed shaft that needs shoring up at the bench. The title screen reads *Phase 3: The River*, so it is built in phases.

## Saving

There are no servers. Accounts are kept in the browser, and every save slot is a separate run, with nothing shared between them.
