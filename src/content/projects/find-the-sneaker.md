Explore a big map, touch a sneaker to collect it, and it is logged in your **Sneakerdex** with its name and rarity.

## How it works

- **100 sneakers in 6 rarities:** Easy (25), Medium (25), Hard (20), Insane (15), Extreme (10) and Soleless (5). Soleless shoes show `???` until you find them.
- **Sneaker Square:** a circular Greek-rotunda hub with a giant spinning sneaker, and ten themed zones branching off it, from City Street to Skate Park to Train Station.
- **Sneakerdex:** a dark-themed collection book with a 4-column grid, rarity badges, search and filters. Found shoes get a tick, undiscovered ones are blacked-out silhouettes.
- **Saved progress:** the collection is stored in Roblox DataStores.
- **Leaderboards:** one for most sneakers found and one for top donors, both backed by ordered DataStores that refresh every minute.
