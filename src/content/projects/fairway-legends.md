A golf game that runs in the browser on a PC or a phone. I rebuilt it from the ground up after studying the Roblox game *Realistic Golf* (Blox Golf) by Sharkie212: the same kind of game, with its own art style, courses and characters. Nothing is copied from Roblox.

## What's in it

- **Swipe swing:** drag down to power up, swipe up to hit. Faster and straighter is better. Tilt the swipe to push or pull the ball, and curve it to draw or fade.
- **Real ball physics:** drag, spin lift, wind, landings that bite into the turf, backspin that pulls the ball back on greens, sloped greens, trees, flagstick hits and lip-outs.
- **Two original courses:** *Timber Valley* (18 holes, par 72, a pine valley) and *Willow Creek* (9 holes, par 36, rolling meadows with a creek).
- **Six golfers,** each with their own look, outfits and stats for power, accuracy, spin and putting.
- **Get around** on foot, in a golf cart, or by teleporting to your ball. Practice mode lets you place the ball anywhere.
- **Modes:** Free Play, Competitive (with leaderboards), Practice, and Online (stroke play or scramble).
- **A shop** with Pro and Tour club sets, balls, putters and outfits. You earn coins by playing.

## How it's built

Plain HTML and JavaScript with no build step. It has to be served by a web server, and it works on a phone too (add it to the home screen for full screen).
