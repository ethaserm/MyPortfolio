A team deathmatch set in the Wild West, inspired by Wild Revolvers. Sheriffs face Cowboys, one hit kills, and the team with the most kills when the timer runs out wins.

## Design

- **Roll-dodge:** **Q** rolls left, **E** rolls right, and **R** rolls forward or back depending on where you're moving. A short cooldown stops spamming, so it's the main way to dodge.
- **Camera:** over-the-shoulder, with a setting to swap which shoulder the crosshair is on. Shots are fired from the camera, not the gun barrel, so you hit what you aim at.
- **Weapon:** a single hitscan revolver for version one.

## The plan

I agreed a build order before writing anything: camera rig, roll-dodge, revolver, Western map, teams, match loop, then UI. The roll-dodge script is written. The rest is still to build.
