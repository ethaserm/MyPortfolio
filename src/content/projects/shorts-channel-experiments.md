Before [Infinite Roads](../infinite-roads/), I tried two Shorts channels built from free tools.

## TheMissingFile: unsolved mysteries

Faceless Shorts. Scripts and image prompts from Gemini, a text-to-speech voice, CapCut for editing and VEED for captions. I fixed the output format: a shocking plain-English fact naming a specific case, with a real number, ending on the part nobody can answer ("Nobody Knows Why", not "Here's Why").

The first four videos, with views as of 2026-04-18:

| Video | Views |
| --- | --- |
| Hotel (vague title) | 10 |
| Dyatlov Pass | 77 |
| Zodiac Killer | 28 |
| MH370: 239 people, Nobody Knows Why | **785** |

What I took from it: name the specific case, include a number, and don't write vague titles like "This Hotel".

## CutLab: satisfying knife-cutting clips

AI clips of knives slicing fruit and soap, with a short fact and ASMR-style sound. I picked the name from four options. When Claude suggested stock footage, I refused ("free!!") and worked out the free clip budget myself: 3 accounts, 2 clips a day each, 8 seconds a clip, so about 3 videos a day.

**This one is a plan, not a result.** My notes have no view or subscriber numbers for it.

## What I learned

Free video tools hit paywalls fast. The daily cap on the clip generator is the bottleneck, auto-captions cap at 10 seconds, and free caption tools add watermarks.
