Use this as the starting point for a game project.

## Suggested sections

- **The pitch:** one sentence on what you do in the game.
- **How it plays:** controls, the core loop, what makes it fun.
- **Made with:** engine, tools, who did the art and sound.
- **Try it:** put the play or download link first in `links` so it becomes the main button.

Add screenshots with `gallery` in the front matter, and a trailer with `video: <YouTube link>`.
