I build apps, games and YouTube videos, mostly working alongside AI tools, and I keep this site as a record of what I make and what I learn along the way.

It started small in April 2026 with simple HTML tools for tracking my routines and money. Since then I've built Roblox games, browser games, a Dynamic Island for Windows, a life-tracking web app, a business making AI tools for UK local businesses, and a stickman survival-story YouTube channel called Infinite Roads.

**How I work:** I try things quickly, write down what I learn, and check that something really works before I call it done. The [Timeline](../timeline/) shows how it's gone so far.
